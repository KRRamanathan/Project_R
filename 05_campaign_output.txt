These are interpretation constraints, not new calculations.

1. Causal assumption under the channel ceiling
   The 216.5 approved/month (100% close) and the 108–162/month at 50–75%
   assume that if an organic/paid captain received the fos_field *experience*,
   they would convert at fos_field's rate. fos_field is in-person recruitment.
   Recruits may already differ (docs in hand, pre-vetted vehicles, agent
   pre-screening, higher commitment). This extract cannot separate selection
   (who fos recruits) from treatment (how they onboard).
   Present as: IF we could fully replicate the fos_field experience with ZERO
   selection difference, this is the CEILING. Not: we will get 216 more/month.

2. Two distinct interventions — do not add them
   C1 guided-capture / provisional activation:
     RC+Insurance uploaded, failed only on capture reasons.
     n=2,048 (~375/mo); self-serve slice 892 (~164/mo).
     Mechanism isolated to blur/OCR/illegible. No fos-selection problem.
     This is the safer lead recommendation.
   Channel-replication (fos experience at scale):
     Whole organic/paid conversion gap, all stages.
     128–256 approved/month at 50–100% of the gap.
     Larger, more interesting, unproven causal assumption — test, don't bank.
   Do not sum 164 + 128. Different captains, different mechanisms.

3. Already solid as stated: Insurance-abandonment channel pattern;
   13 post-Fitness nudges = not a usable program; CAC cannot be computed.

4. DOES THE 'BIG WIN, SCALE 5×' CLAIM HOLD?
====================================================================================================
  Claim: CAMP_WA_002 is a big win for onboarding completion and should be scaled 5x.

  What we can defend in a deck:
    Among mature captains with a delivered CAMP_WA_002 message (n=7,387):
    clicked  28.29% (867/3,065) W95% [26.72,29.91]
    not      29.80% (1,288/4,322) W95% [28.46,31.18]
    Δ=-1.51pp  Wald95% [-3.61, +0.58]  z=-1.41 p=0.1584

  Number I would put in a deck with my name on it:
    Completion lift attributable to clicking CAMP_WA_002: 0 pp.
    Best estimate of the click contrast: -1.5pp
    95% CI [-3.6, +0.6] pp, p=0.16.
    Confidence in 'no large positive click-lift': HIGH (n=7,387 delivered,
    CI rules out lifts larger than about the upper Wald bound).
    Confidence in 'the campaign has zero effect on anyone': LOW — this is not
    an RCT; we did not test send vs no-send among eligible captains, and 30%
    of recipients got another campaign.

  The naive recipient vs non-recipient gap (29% vs 11% approved, mature) is
  NOT a campaign effect. Recipients are selected after they have already
  cleared RC. Scaling 5x would either (i) message more of the same RC-cleared
  pool, where clicking shows no completion lift, or (ii) expand to people who
  have not cleared RC, a population this campaign has not been shown to work on.

  Verdict: the 'big win, scale it 5x' claim does NOT hold for onboarding
  completion. I would not fund a 5x scale on this evidence. A test I would
  fund: randomized send vs no-send among RC-cleared self-serve captains,
  exclusive of other campaigns, with Aadhaar-pass and approved as endpoints.
