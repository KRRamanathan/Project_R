Controlled pilot design
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Population: new organic_app and paid_digital signups (the self-serve
  channels behind the gap). Exclude fos_field, referral, gc_telecalling.
  Assignment: individual-level randomization at signup (or at first
  doc-upload intent) to assisted onboarding support vs current self-serve.
  Treatment: time-bounded human assist (video/centre/call) aimed at the
  fos operational experience — document capture and stage progression —
  NOT a change in who is recruited. That is the point of the test:
  hold the signup pool fixed, vary assist.
  Control: status-quo self-serve funnel, same eligibility and docs.
  Do not send CAMP_WA_002 differentially across arms (see §5).
  Primary endpoint (confirmatory): P(approved) within a pre-registered
  mature window (signup age > ~16 days, matching the empirical cut).
  Secondary: P(RC pass | reached RC); P(cleared all required docs);
  time-to-RC-pass; never-attempt rate.
  Guardrails: no peeking at 128–256 as a success bar; success is a
  pre-registered MDE on the randomized contrast. Stratify by channel
  (organic vs paid) and device_tier.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Approximate sample size (two-arm, 1:1, two-sided α=0.05, 80% power)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Formula: n_per_arm = (z_{α/2}+z_β)^2 [p_c(1-p_c)+p_t(1-p_t)] / δ^2
  Control rates locked to observed self-serve event-funnel.
  fos's +6.4pp approved / +35.9pp RC gaps are NOT the design MDE —
  those mix selection and operations. Design for a smaller, operationally
  plausible lift.

  endpoint                                   p_ctrl       MDE     n/arm   n total
  approved (primary)                         14.99%     3.0pp     2,398     4,795
  approved (primary)                         14.99%     5.0pp       902     1,804
  RC pass | reached RC                       70.43%     5.0pp     1,236     2,471
  RC pass | reached RC                       70.43%     8.0pp       463       926
  Attrition/immature: inflate n by the immature share (~signup last
  16 days of the experiment calendar). For a ~2-month enrollment,
  budget ~15–20% extra. No clustering inflation if assignment is
  individual; inflate if assist is centre-batched.
  Practical read: ~900/arm detects a 5pp approved lift; ~2,400/arm
  for a 3pp lift. That is a real experiment, not a 50-person ops trial.
  A null at 5pp would reject 'fos rates are portable to organic/paid
  signups' as an operating assumption, which is the decision this
  test is for.

    payout       ₹/leg     sample ₹/mo   vs rest-sub gap   vs worst-core gap
       30%       105.0         254,316             +73.4               +25.2

       70%       245.0         593,405            +213.4              +165.2
  'vs gap' = payout per eligible leg minus the net-cycle gap (₹).
  Positive ⇒ payout more than fills that comparison gap on a per-trip
  basis (overpay relative to that benchmark); negative ⇒ underfills.
  Gap-equivalent share of local fare: ₹31.6 = 9.0% of fare (restore rest-suburban net); ₹79.8 = 22.8% of fare (match worst×city_core — geography, not ARA's job).
  All three requested cells (30/50/70%) overshoot BOTH gaps:
    30% of fare = ₹105.0 vs ₹31.6 rest-sub
    and vs ₹79.8 worst-core. ARA at 30%+ of fare
    overpays relative to closing the overnight-suburban penalty.
    The evidence-grounded payout to close rest-suburban net is ~9% of
    fare, not 30–70%. Report 30/50/70 as requested; do not bank them
    as the efficient price.

Naive new-headcount alternative
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A 'hire more captains to sit the airport overnight' plan faces the
  same two independent penalties Step 7 documented for incumbents:
    (1) suburban backhaul (distance ~23 km vs ~13 km city_core, all hours)
    (2) overnight return-fare collapse (−8 to −9pp in every drop type)
  Mix does not shift overnight, so new captains are not a mix-shift
  lever. They inherit worst×suburban net ₹24.9. Staffing the mean
  deficit of ~24 online captains vs daytime does not
  change trip geography or return probability. ARA prices the deadhead;
  hiring does not. No CAC field exists — cannot convert headcount to ₹.
  Qualitative: do not substitute 'recruit N airport captains' for ARA
  without a product that changes overnight-suburban expected net.
