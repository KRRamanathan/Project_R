3 / 6 · The fix

One recommendation, two sub-actions. DL and Insurance still gate.

C1a — RC provisional activation

  • 1,637 people (~300/month). In-person RC within 10 days.
  • RC can defer: vehicle identity, no pre-ride passenger liability.
  • 60% show-up × 74% pass → ~134/month. Show-up is unobserved (40–80% band: ~89–188).
  • Legal/T&S required. If they must still finish Aadhaar→Insurance before a ride, historical P(approved | passed RC) = 27% and C1a shrinks toward ~36/month. That is the Legal ask.
  • Ops: ~180 centre visits/month at 60% show-up → about one FTE, ₹40–60k/month (assumed 12–15 checks/day). Not in the extract.

C1b — Insurance capture UX

  • 411 people. Blur/OCR feedback at upload.
  • Must still clear before the first ride.
  • Insurance retry 67→74→76% × 91% approved-if-cleared → ~46–52/month.
  • No deferral sign-off. Ship without waiting on Legal for C1a.
Metric: monthly approved, split by cohort.
R2A: 98.7% of approved take a first order.
After C1b: same UX on other docs — measure, don’t add to 180.

4 / 6 · Airport

Nights + compounding economics. Not “hire near the airport.”

When: airport unfulfilled 40% vs ~3% elsewhere. 84% of that pain is 21:00–03:59. ~13 captains online vs ~37 by day. Surge is already high — a non-price constraint.

After the trip: suburban cancel 24% vs city-core 12%. Overnight return-in-20m: suburban 10.7% vs city-core 47%. Mix of suburban vs city-core does not change overnight. Two independent penalties, not a mix-shift.

Net ₹/cycle: worst×suburban ₹24.9 vs rest×suburban ₹56.5 vs worst×city-core ₹104.7.

ARA payout — derived, not % of fare

  • Pays only unpaid suburban night completed legs (89.31%).
  • ₹31.6 population gap ÷ 0.8931 = ₹35.4 / eligible leg.
  • 80 / 100 / 120%: ₹28.3 / 35.4 / 42.5 → sample ~₹69k / ₹86k / ₹103k a month.
  • 30% of the ₹350 fare (₹105) overpays. Do not use it.
  • Target rest-suburban parity, not city-core (geography).
No blanket hiring. New captains inherit both penalties. Headcount only if a 4-week ARA run-rate is not falling.
Trips are sampled — ₹/month is a design price, not a city P&L line.4 / 6
